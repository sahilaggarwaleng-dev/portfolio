What I worked on

  • Drawings the build team works from: flat harness drawings and 3D CAD reference models that technicians built and installed harnesses from, across 10+ conversion builds.
  • Redesign for cost: with the ME team, I converted bent sheet metal parts in legacy SolidWorks drawings to welded assemblies and wrote the new drawings and welding instructions, cutting EV costs by $10K.
  • Packaging: sub-assembly layouts for electrical junction boxes and sheet metal components, balancing fit between wiring, enclosure and mounting constraints.
  • Documentation technicians actually use: build-sequencing and installation guides across 4–5 bus builds, including interactive eDrawings with embedded BOMs, so a technician can click a part to identify it.
  • On the floor: vehicle commissioning and EE testing across the conversion fleet, including drilling and part installation.
